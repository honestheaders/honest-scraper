# Privacy policy – Honest Scraper

Published by Honest Tools. Last updated: 2026-10-11.

Honest Scraper does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any data.

- It reads a web page only when you click its toolbar button or its right-click menu item, and only in that tab (Chrome's `activeTab`).
- What it reads (the rows and columns you ask for) stays in the extension window on your computer. It is copied to your clipboard or saved as a file only when you choose to.
- It makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no ads, no remote code.
- Session storage is used only to pass the right-clicked element to the extension window. Chrome clears it when the browser closes.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/honestheaders/honest-scraper/issues
